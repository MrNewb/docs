# Hey.

Welcome to the **MrNewb Scripts** docs.

I write FiveM resources. Before that I ran a server, so I know the part that never makes it into an install guide: five inventories, a start order that looks fine until it isn’t, and an hour of wondering whether the script is broken or you missed a line in `fxmanifest`. Navigating this ecosystem can be a bit of a struggle. I’ve been on that side of the console.

A lot of that time was **MulletVille** — my city, my mess, my late nights. I was heavily involved in **Ozark RP** too, even though I didn’t own it. **AllGood RP** is another one I put a lot of hours into. Those servers are why these scripts exist: give people a reason to log in besides a job menu.

The work is here to make cities more interactive, fun, and worth staying in — a hive to check on, a shop to visit, a plate to put on a car. Things that still feel good on a quiet ten-person city, and don’t fall over when the player list is long.

I haven’t always been a sellout. Most of these scripts are free, and that’s still the work I care about. The paid ones are how I buy pizza and help with bills while I work a normal 8–4.

Most of it starts after ox_lib and Newb_Bridge. When you need a start order, an item list, or an export, open the resource on the left.
