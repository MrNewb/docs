A pocket pet. Players carry one item, use it, and a little handheld pops up. They hatch an egg, name the pet, feed it, play with it, and try not to neglect it. Runs on [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge).

[Discord](https://discord.gg/mrnewbscripts) · [Configuration](/mrnewbdigitalpets/install/configuration)

## What players get

- **One item, one pet.** Species, name, stats, coins, and level all travel with the item. Trade it, stash it, lose it, and the pet goes with it
- **A real routine.** Hunger, thirst, happiness, energy, and hygiene drain over time. Feed, water, hug, walk, clean, give medicine, or put the pet to bed
- **Play.** A handful of small arcade games on the handheld's screen earn happiness and coins
- **A coin shop.** Toys and fortune cookies cost pet coins, never real money
- **Company.** Players with their toys open near each other can visit and swap small gifts
- **Things to do outside.** The pet asks to see a landmark and gives a reward when you get there. Some zones on the map also make certain species happy
- **A pedometer.** Optional step counting while on foot, which boosts the Walk button. It only lasts for the session
- **Neglect has a cost.** Leave a pet alone for long enough and it will die

## What staff get

An in-game setup panel, `/digitalpetssetup`. Hatch time, drain rates, care values, rarity odds, the shop, outings, spots, and the handheld prop are all editable there, and saving applies for everyone right away. No restarts.

## Good to know

- Foods, drinks, eggs, toys, and fortune cookies are **not** inventory items. Only `digital_pet` is. You never need to register anything else
- There are no exports. To give a pet, add the `digital_pet` item the normal way
- The Bee Care game is built into the toy. It does not connect to MrNewbBeeKeeping
