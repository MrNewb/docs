---

- [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge)
- ox_lib
- [Server artifact and OneSync](/getting-started/download#check-your-artifact)
- Framework via the bridge (cash)
- An [inventory Newb_Bridge supports](/newb_bridge/supported#inventory), including `one_inventory` (sell / buy / smelt items)
- A [target Newb_Bridge supports](/newb_bridge/supported#target) (shop peds + foundry **box zones**)

Items are **not** usable. The script does not register item use. Names in `itemlist` and `MeltableItems` must exist in your inventory. Shipped PNGs are in `[INSTALL]/images/` (filename = item name).
