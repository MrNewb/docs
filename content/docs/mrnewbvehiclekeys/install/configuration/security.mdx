## Vehicle security

Equipment that protects a car, fitted from the inventory. Everything here is on the **Vehicle security** page of `/setupkeys`: fitting, who can fit each item, alarms, GPS, the keypad PIN, the scanner and the staff log.

The items are in [Inventory items](/mrnewbvehiclekeys/install/inventory), and their pictures in [Item images](/mrnewbvehiclekeys/install/inventory-images#security-item-pictures). The locksmith NPC and the locksmith computer sell them from the same price list as the rest of the catalogue.

### Fitting

Use the item next to a parked, registered car with its engine off and nobody in it. A progress bar runs (**Fitting time**), the item is used up, and a failed or cancelled fit keeps it.

Who may fit it is set per item on the **Who can fit it** card. The shipped lists are `mechanic` and `locksmith` for the alarms, cut-off relay, keypad lock and GPS, so only those jobs can fit them, and they can do it on any registered car without a key. Empty an item's list and anyone holding the item can fit it, but then they must hold a key for that car (or be an admin), so nobody can fit equipment on a stranger's car. **On duty only** makes the job lists count only on duty.

The GPS jammer and the removal kit are open to anyone by default, because they are tampering tools.

### Alarms

| Tier | Who hears it |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | Every car, no item. The siren, plus the police alert from the dispatch settings |
| Linked (`alarm_module_linked`) | The siren, and the owner when they are online, with a flashing blip. The owner testing their own car does not alert themselves. `/clearalarm` removes the blip, and it also goes after a minute or when the owner is back at the car |
| Dispatch (`alarm_module_dispatch`) | Everything above, and police dispatch to the jobs picked under **Dispatch alarm goes to** |

Lockpicking sounds the alarm as the pick goes in, and hotwiring and carjacking as they start. **Wait between alerts** stops one car raising an alert over and over. A jammed car sends no wireless alert.

### Immobilisers and the keypad PIN

An immobiliser (`ignition_cutoff_relay`) blocks hotwiring, and a valid key is still needed. A keypad lock (`keypad_cutoff_module`) does that and also asks for a four digit PIN at every engine start. It turns remote start off. The PIN is chosen when the keypad lock is fitted, typed on a pad that looks like the hotwire minigame, and changed with `/enginepin`.

- **PIN entry time** is how long the pad stays open.
- **Wrong tries** in a row sound the alarm and lock the pad for the **Retry delay**.
- A job vehicle, a car shared with a job, never has a keypad. One cannot be fitted to it, nobody can set up or change a PIN on it, and nobody is asked for one, so a job's car cannot be locked out by a PIN its members were not given. The **Skip the PIN in job vehicles** switch turns this off.
- A sold car has its PIN cleared, and the first key holder to sit in it chooses a new one.
- The PIN is kept as a salted hash. Four digits only have 10,000 combinations, so what protects the car is the retry limit and the alarm, not the hash.

### GPS

`gps_tracker_module` fits the same tracker as the transmitter. It belongs to the car's registered owner, and the scanner or `gps_signal_jammer` can silence it. `gps_signal_jammer` silences it for the **Jam time**, which also blocks the receiver ping and the wireless side of the alarm. `tracker_extraction_kit` removes it. **Use up the jammer** and **Use up the removal kit** choose whether they are used up.

### The scanner

The keyfob scanner can scan a car and then bypass or jam it. See [The scanner device](/mrnewbvehiclekeys/install/configuration/locksmith#the-scanner-device).

### Staff log

Fitting, jamming, tracker removal, PIN lockouts, alarms, scanner scans and tries, and admin changes are written through ox_lib's logger. The **Staff log** card has the on and off switch and the Discord webhook, and **Do not log these** (with Advanced on) skips single events.
