<Callout type="info">
  You need Newb_Bridge, ox_lib, and a framework and inventory the bridge supports. Vehicle keys and fuel come through the bridge too. Start VehicleRentals after all of them. The full list is on [Requirements](/mrnewbvehiclerentals/requirements).
</Callout>

### Unzip it [step]

Put the `MrNewbVehicleRentals` folder in your `resources` folder. oxmysql is not needed.

### Add the items [step]

There is one item, `rental_paperwork`. Paste it from [Item setup](/mrnewbvehiclerentals/install/inventory), then copy its picture from [Item images](/mrnewbvehiclerentals/install/inventory-images). The paperwork is only a token, and cars are returned from the rental menu.

### Add it to server.cfg [step]

```cfg
ensure ox_lib
ensure qbx_core          # or qb-core / es_extended
ensure ox_inventory      # or your inventory
ensure Newb_Bridge
ensure MrNewbVehicleRentals
```

### Configure it [step]

Open `resource/open/shared/config.lua` for the agencies and map blips. There is no deposit: the price is charged to the bank and refunded to the bank on a valid return. Every setting is on [Configuration](/mrnewbvehiclerentals/install/configuration).

### Restart and check [step]

Restart the server after config or item changes.
