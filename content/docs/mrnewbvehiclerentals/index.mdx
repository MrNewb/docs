Rental desks. A ped or a prop, a `rental_paperwork` item, and they bring it back close by. Cars, bikes, boats, whatever you stick in the list. Runs on [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge/getting-started).

[GitHub](https://github.com/MrNewb/MrNewbVehicleRentals) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/mrnewbscripts) · [Configuration](/mrnewbvehiclerentals/install/configuration) · [Preview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uJylvMP8_PY)

<YouTubePoster id="uJylvMP8_PY" title="MrNewbVehicleRentals preview" className="resource-video" />

## Features

- A desk can be a ped or a prop, with its own stock and spawn pads
- Rent charges the bank, hands out keys, and gives one `rental_paperwork`
- Return refunds the bank, pulls the keys, and deletes the vehicle

Agencies, paperwork metadata, and the file map: [Configuration](/mrnewbvehiclerentals/install/configuration). Item paste: [Item setup](/mrnewbvehiclerentals/install/inventory). Icon: [Item images](/mrnewbvehiclerentals/install/inventory-images).
