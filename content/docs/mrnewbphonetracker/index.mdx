A tracker you can lock to certain jobs. They type a phone number on the GPS, and if that player’s online they get a waypoint. Runs on [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge/getting-started).

[GitHub](https://github.com/MrNewb/MrNewbPhoneTracker) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/mrnewbscripts) · [Configuration](/mrnewbphonetracker/install/configuration)

## Features

- Job and grade gate on the usable `tracker` item
- Handheld GPS overlay. Digits only, then a waypoint if that number is online
- A miss stays on the glass. A hit closes the device and drops a timed blip

Phone lookups live under [Compatibility](/mrnewbphonetracker/compatibility). Config and the use flow: [Configuration](/mrnewbphonetracker/install/configuration). Item paste: [Item setup](/mrnewbphonetracker/install/inventory). Icon: [Item images](/mrnewbphonetracker/install/inventory-images).
