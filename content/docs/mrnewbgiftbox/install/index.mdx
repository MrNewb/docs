<Callout type="info">
  You need Newb_Bridge, ox_lib, and a framework and inventory the bridge supports. Start GiftBox after all of them. The full list is on [Requirements](/mrnewbgiftbox/requirements).
</Callout>

### Unzip it [step]

Put the `MrNewbGiftBox` folder in your `resources` folder. oxmysql is not needed.

### Add the items [step]

Every gift box you define needs a matching usable item, and every reward item has to exist in your inventory. Paste the shipped item from [Item setup](/mrnewbgiftbox/install/inventory), then copy its picture from [Item images](/mrnewbgiftbox/install/inventory-images).

### Add it to server.cfg [step]

```cfg
ensure ox_lib
ensure qbx_core          # or qb-core / es_extended
ensure ox_inventory      # or your inventory
ensure Newb_Bridge
ensure MrNewbGiftBox
```

### Configure it [step]

Open `resource/open/server/config.lua`. It is server-only. Boxes, rewards, odds and money extras are explained on [Configuration](/mrnewbgiftbox/install/configuration).

### Restart and check [step]

Restart the server after config or item changes.
