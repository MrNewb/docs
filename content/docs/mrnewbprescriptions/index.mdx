A full prescription loop. A doctor writes a script, the patient takes the paper to a pharmacy, a pharmacist bottles it, and the patient pays at the counter. Add insurance, a forgery desk, addiction, and overdoses, and you have a pharmacy that gives your medical and criminal players plenty to do. Runs on [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge).

[Discord](https://discord.gg/mrnewbscripts) · [How it plays](/mrnewbprescriptions/guide) · [Commands](/mrnewbprescriptions/install/commands) · [Exports](/mrnewbprescriptions/exports/server-exports)

## What's in the box

- **Prescription pad.** Doctors write a script for a nearby patient: medication, doses, a note, and a signature. The patient gets a paper they can actually read
- **Pharmacies.** Run by players (clock in, bottle, ring up customers) or by an NPC pharmacist when nobody is working
- **Bottling bench.** A small drag-and-drop minigame and a seal step. Ingredients come out of the pharmacy's stock room
- **Cash register.** Charge a nearby customer. They approve it on their screen. Insurance copays apply automatically
- **Supply runs.** Staff order stock from the tablet and drive the delivery themselves
- **Pharmacy tablet.** Search records, print a medical record, flag or revoke a prescription
- **Insurance.** Three plans, a card item, and an agent ped. You can also plug in m-Insurance or your own
- **Thirteen medications** with their own effects, and room for as many of your own as you like
- **Addiction, withdrawal, and overdose.** Use the same thing too often and it catches up. Narkhan spray brings someone back
- **Forgery.** An underground desk where people alter real scripts or fake new ones
- **Shoplifting.** Players can rummage pharmacy shelves for over-the-counter items. Police get pinged
- **Envious shots.** A cough syrup shot you mix yourself, with a very real downside
- **Patient chart.** `/mymedication` shows what you are holding, what is unfilled, and who prescribed it

## For staff

Everything is set up from `/prescriptionssetup`, an in-game panel. Saving applies to everyone immediately. Station locations are placed by standing where you want them. There is no SQL file to import.
