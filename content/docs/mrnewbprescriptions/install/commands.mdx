## Everyone

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/mymedication` | Opens your chart: bottles you have, scripts not yet filled, and your doctor history. Needs `PatientHistory` enabled with `useCommand` on |

Players can share their chart with someone standing close. The other person gets a prompt and has 30 seconds to accept.

## Doctors

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/prescription` | Opens the write pad. Only registered when the pad item is **not** the way to open it (`useItem = false`), or when `allowCommandWithItem = true`. You can rename it with `PrescriptionPad.command` |

Most doctors just use the `prescriptionpad` item.

## Admins

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/prescriptionssetup` | Opens the in-game setup |
| `/staffdebug` | Copies a short Newb_Bridge provider report to your clipboard for support. Needs `Utility.Debug = true` |

## Test commands

Each one is off until you flip its switch in `Config.Utility`, and all of them should stay off on a live server.

| Command | Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/testprescription` | `TestPrescriptionCommand` | Opens a sample prescription paper |
| `/testdoctorcreate` | `TestDoctorCreateCommand` | Opens the doctor write pad |
| `/testbottling` | `TestBottlingCommand` | Opens the bottling minigame |
| `/checkaddiction` | `TestAddictionCommand` | Shows your addiction status |
| `/testaddiction [item]` | `TestAddictionCommand` | Forces one addiction use. Defaults to `oxy` |
| `/testmedshare` | `TestMedShareCommand` | Previews the share confirmation prompt |

<Callout type="warning">
  With `Debug = true`, the clocked-in check on billing, supply runs, and the panic button is skipped. Do not leave it on.
</Callout>
