---

- [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge). It handles the framework, inventory, target, notifications, dispatch, and boss menu
- ox_lib and oxmysql
- [Server artifact and OneSync](/getting-started/download#check-your-artifact)
- A framework and inventory Newb_Bridge supports. QBCore, Qbox, and ESX all work. The shipped item files are for ox_inventory, with QB-style examples for everything else
- A **pharmacy job** in your framework. Without one the pharmacies run in NPC-only mode. See [Pharmacy job](/mrnewbprescriptions/install/pharmacy-job)
- The `sprunk` item, only if you want Envious shots. It ships with stock ox_inventory

An ambulance job is optional but recommended. Med heals, insurance on hospital bills, and clearing overdoses on revive all hook into it. Supported: qb-ambulancejob, esx_ambulancejob, qbx_ambulancejob with qbx_medical, wasabi_ambulance_v2, plus ak47, brutal, and osp. See [Ambulance jobs](/mrnewbprescriptions/ambulance-jobs).

Pharmacy MLOs are optional. The shipped store layouts load only when their MLO is running: `kiiya_pillbox` and `moreo_pharmacy`. Any other MLO needs a store file of its own, which is quick to copy. See [Configuration](/mrnewbprescriptions/install/configuration#adding-a-pharmacy).
