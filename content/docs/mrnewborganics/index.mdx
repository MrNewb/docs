## Features

- Fields for apples, pineapples and lettuce. Walk into a field and only the plants that are available spawn
- A picked plant disappears for everyone in the field until the restore timer runs out (two hours by default)
- A plant is a spawned prop when it has a model, and a sphere zone when it does not
- Picks and sales are checked, rate limited and logged on the server
- Farmers market peds that pay the bank
