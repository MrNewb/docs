Pick fruit and vegetables, then sell them at a farmers market. Runs on [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge/getting-started).

[GitHub](https://github.com/MrNewb/MrNewbOrganics) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/mrnewbscripts) · [Configuration](/mrnewborganics/install/configuration)

## Features

- Harvest sites for apples, pineapples, and lettuce
- A site is a spawned prop when it has a model, and a sphere zone when it doesn’t
- Farmers market peds that pay the bank

Pick types, the market, and cooldowns: [Configuration](/mrnewborganics/install/configuration). Item names: [Item setup](/mrnewborganics/install/inventory). Icons: [Item images](/mrnewborganics/install/inventory-images).

No exports and no commands.
