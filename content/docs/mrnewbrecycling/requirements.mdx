- [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge) (framework, inventory, target, notifications, text UI, prop placer, and gizmo)
- ox_lib, oxmysql
- [Server artifact and OneSync](/getting-started/download#check-your-artifact)
- An inventory Newb_Bridge already supports

QBCore, Qbox, and ESX work through the bridge. You do not edit the framework.

The NUI ships in `web/build/`. Props and the running sound ship in `stream/`. Do not start that folder as its own resource.

Keep the folder named `MrNewb_Recycling`.
