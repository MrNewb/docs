Scrap recyclers. Players load items, run the machine, and collect materials. Staff place and configure every machine in `/recyclingsetup`. Runs on [Newb_Bridge](/newb_bridge).

[Discord](https://discord.gg/mrnewbscripts) · [Commands](/mrnewbrecycling/install/commands) · [Exports](/mrnewbrecycling/exports)

## Features

- Prop, ped, or box-zone stations. Each one has its own distance, load radius, and slot counts
- Types (`basic`, `industrial`, `advanced`) with efficiency, speed, and their own item lists
- Recipes: seconds per item, weighted outputs, optional scale-by-condition
- One player on a machine at a time. Only the player who loaded an item can take it back out
- Optional murder mode, off by default
- Settings save live. No restart after a panel save

This resource adds no items of its own. Seed recipes use `phone`, `radio`, and `lockpick` in, and `plastic`, `metalscrap`, and `copper` out. Those have to already exist in your inventory.

Setup: [Configuration](/mrnewbrecycling/install/configuration). Props: [Anticheat models](/mrnewbrecycling/anticheat).
