<Callout type="warning">
  WeaponTints is **not supported on Kora**. Its items are ox_inventory item definitions that rely on `server.export` and `allowArmed`, and `kora_inventory` does not run those. Use ox_inventory for this resource.
</Callout>

The normal steps are on [Installation](/mrnewbweapontints/install).
