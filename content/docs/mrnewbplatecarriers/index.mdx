## Features

- Each carrier keeps its condition, tier and serial number in item metadata, so the armor moves with the inventory
- Five tiers: Pegasus, Merryweather, FIB, NOOSE and Armstrong. The tier sets the carrier's maximum armor
- A plate HUD shows the worn carrier and its plates. Players can toggle it, and it hides when the pause menu opens
- Players manage worn and stored carriers from one menu, which explains why a carrier cannot be repaired
- Repair plates repair a worn carrier in place, with an animation. A repair that would go over the carrier's maximum is refused
- Heavier tiers slow the player a little. This can be turned off
- A police serial check lists the carriers in a bag or the vest worn by the nearest person, with a short owner history
- A carrier worn when a player leaves is restored on login, including its armor and clothing

## How it plays

Use a carrier item to put it on. Its condition becomes the player's armor, and it drains as the player is hit. Use a repair plate to restore it. With no carrier worn, using a repair plate opens the menu so you can pick which carrier in your bag to repair.

The menu opens with the `carriers` command or the menu key. Players can take the vest off from the menu, with `/removevest` or its key. See [Commands](/mrnewbplatecarriers/install/commands) for the keys.

## What the server checks

The server checks repairs and serial lookups:

- A repair plate is only used when the carrier ends up at or under its maximum. Otherwise the plate is kept and the player is told the current condition.
- The serial check needs one of the allowed jobs, and looking at another player's vest needs that player to be within 5 meters.
