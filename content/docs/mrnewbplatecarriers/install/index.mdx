<Callout type="info">
  You need Newb_Bridge, ox_lib, and an inventory that stores per-item metadata. Start PlateCarriers after all of them. The full list is on [Requirements](/mrnewbplatecarriers/requirements).
</Callout>

### Unzip it [step]

Put the `MrNewbPlateCarriers` folder in your `resources` folder. oxmysql is not needed.

### Add the items [step]

Paste the list for your inventory from [Item setup](/mrnewbplatecarriers/install/inventory), then copy the pictures from [Item images](/mrnewbplatecarriers/install/inventory-images). If you are upgrading, keep your existing repair plate names.

### Add it to server.cfg [step]

```cfg
ensure ox_lib
ensure qbx_core          # or qb-core / es_extended
ensure ox_inventory      # or your inventory
ensure Newb_Bridge
ensure MrNewbPlateCarriers
```

### Configure it [step]

Open `resource/open/shared/config.lua` for the carrier tiers, repair plates, clothing, the HUD and the serial check. Every setting is on [Configuration](/mrnewbplatecarriers/install/configuration). Optional Buckshot_Crafting recipes are in `[INSTALL]/crafting/README.md`.

### Restart and check [step]

Restart the server after config or item changes.
